Test-set human check

300 cards on ten pages. The card images are personal data: keep this folder private.
How it works
  1. You see one card image and type what is printed: EPIC number, house number, age, relation and gender. Nothing from any earlier transcription is shown.
  2. When you press Check, any field where your entry differs from the first transcription is shown side by side. Look at the image again and choose.
  3. Your first entry and your final choice are both saved, so the agreement rate can be reported.

Typing the names yourself is the stronger check. Showing them is faster and is reported as "verified", not "independently transcribed".

Enter moves to the next field. Type exactly what is printed, including punctuation. If a glyph cannot be decided at this size, choose your best reading and tick "cannot tell".
All cards are done.

Press Save file and then run:
node paper/analysis/human_check_apply.mjs <the saved file>
